Quality policy

As part of our ISO 9001 certification, our quality policy guides everything we do. Our commitment to quality stands on two pillars.

  • Keep our promises — Deliver products that meet the agreed requirements, on time, satisfying customer needs and expectations.
  • Continuously improve — Monitor and continuously improve our business, products, services, organisation and people's performance.

DMAIC quality control

  • Define — Lock the spec, customer expectations and acceptance criteria.
  • Measure — IQC, first-piece inspection, in-process data capture.
  • Analyze — Root-cause analysis on non-conforming items, CAR issued.
  • Improve — Process improvement and controlled rework to prevent recurrence.
  • Control — FQC final inspection, IPQC patrol, last verification before stock.
