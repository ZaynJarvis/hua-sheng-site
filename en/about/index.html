Founded in 1989, built for urban public facilities and advanced manufacturing

From Guangzhou, HuaSheng connects design, metal manufacturing, installation support and project delivery for public transport facilities, bus stop shelters, urban furniture, metal furniture, outdoor pavilions and custom metal products.

From industry and trade to a diversified industrial ecosystem

HuaSheng was founded in Guangzhou in 1989. The name HuaSheng was personally inscribed by Hua Guofeng, a historical mark that became part of the company's long-term identity.

Over more than three decades, HuaSheng has developed a coordinated business framework covering advanced industrial manufacturing and installation, industrial park development and management, commercial real estate development and management, and new energy development and management.

In advanced manufacturing, HuaSheng continues to invest in the Yinyisi Advanced Intelligent Manufacturing Industrial Park in Huadu, Guangzhou, combining modern workshops, staff dormitories, efficient production lines and smart manufacturing facilities.

HuaSheng serves more than 100 cities and regions worldwide. Its products include public transport shelters and bus stops, urban furniture, metal furniture, outdoor pavilions and kitchens, metal household goods and custom metal components.

With project experience, manufacturing equipment and quality systems, HuaSheng has built long-term relationships with White Horse Outdoor, IKEA, Belson Outdoors, Valeo, BYD Backyard, Ladenbau Schmidt and other clients and partners.

From Guangzhou's first generation of modern bus shelters to Beijing LED bus route signs, Shanghai smart shelters, Shenzhen Qianhai and Futian smart shelters, and public transport projects in Saudi Arabia and Qatar, HuaSheng turns small urban facilities into deliverable engineering products.

Manufacturing, industrial park, property and new energy

  • Advanced manufacturing & installation — Design, fabrication, packing and installation support for transport shelters, urban furniture, outdoor pavilions, metal household products and custom components.
  • Industrial park development — The Yinyisi Advanced Intelligent Manufacturing Industrial Park in Huadu combines modern workshops, dormitories, production lines and management systems.
  • Commercial real estate management — Industrial and commercial spaces support manufacturing, storage, showroom and service functions over the long term.
  • New energy development — New business around energy infrastructure, network terminal cabinets, hydrogen-related facilities and cooling systems for communication base stations.

From city projects to global brand supply chains

  • White Horse Outdoor / Belson Outdoors — Long-term participation in overseas outdoor public facilities and urban furniture supply, with structure, packing and delivery support.
  • IKEA Sweden — Stable welding, surface treatment and quality-control capability for functional home and metal storage products.
  • Valeo — Metal manufacturing and project collaboration for industrial customers, with emphasis on delivery stability and process quality.
  • BYD Backyard — Outdoor kitchens and gazebos that extend HuaSheng's metal fabrication capability into modern backyard living.
  • Ladenbau Schmidt — Stainless steel products and manufacturing support for Swiss retail spaces serving local systems such as Coop and Migros.
  • Domestic urban facility clients — Public transport, wayfinding, information screen and public-space projects across Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Wuxi, Xi'an and Chongqing.

Milestones

  • 1993 · Shelter manufacturing begins — The metal materials business takes shape and builds Guangzhou's first generation of modern bus shelters.
  • 2001 · International customers — Long-term manufacturing cooperation with IKEA and other overseas customers brings HuaSheng into global supply chains.
  • 2008 · National project experience — Public facility work around Beijing Olympic venues strengthens large urban project delivery capability.
  • 2014 · Overseas transport projects — Projects in Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Nepal and more build cross-border manufacturing and installation experience.
  • 2020 · Smart transit systems — Beijing LED route signs, Shenzhen smart shelters and Xi'an LCD shelters push products toward intelligent operation.
  • 2025 · Advanced manufacturing park — The Yinyisi Advanced Intelligent Manufacturing Industrial Park in Huadu continues to upgrade production and management capacity.
