Bus stop shelters & smart shelters

Custom bus stop shelters and bus shelters — from compact single-bay stops to long multi-bay shelters — plus smart shelters with integrated LED/LCD route displays, lighting, Wi-Fi and USB charging. Shelters ship as modular, flat-packed units classified by assembly sequence for local installation.

SpecificationSummary
MaterialsHot-dip galvanized steel, 304/201 stainless steel or aluminium profiles; tempered safety glass, polycarbonate or metal canopies; outdoor powder coating or electrophoresis.
Typical applicationsCity transit routes, smart-city stops and transit-advertising programmes — from Guangzhou, Beijing, Shanghai and Shenzhen to Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Nepal, New Zealand, Korea and Romania.
Spec summarySingle-bay to multi-bay layouts engineered to your drawings or local transit standards; ISO 9001, bus shelter design patents and structural utility patents.

Advertising light boxes & displays
