What a 50,000-m² metal fabrication workshop inside our 120,000-m² base can actually do

Raw stock to packed container — every step closed inside our own plant.

Five workshops

  • Hardware fabrication — Laser cutting, CNC shearing and punching, bending, slotting, water jet. Forming cold-rolled steel, stainless, aluminium and galvanised plate.
  • Riveting & welding — Robotic auto-welding paired with skilled manual welding. For both structural and visible parts.
  • Surface treatment — Digital automatic spray line — pre-treatment, electrophoresis, powder coating, baking.
  • Clean assembly — Final assembly, electrical and accessory integration, pre-shipment quality check in a clean environment.
  • Loading & dispatch — Totem-style container loading to maximise utilisation, plus overseas installation guidance.

Key equipment

  • 400W laser cutter
  • Robotic auto-welding cells
  • Digital auto-spray line
  • CNC bending / shearing
  • CNC punching machines
  • Tube bender / slotter
  • Water jet cutter
  • Electrophoresis pre-treatment line

Standard process

  • Design & process review — Engineering team reviews drawings and proposes process
  • IQC incoming inspection — Steel, aluminium and accessories inspected before stocking
  • Cutting & forming — Laser cutting, punching, bending, tube bending
  • Welding & riveting — Robotic plus manual welding
  • Surface treatment — Pre-treatment, electrophoresis, coating, baking
  • Assembly & commissioning — Electrical, accessory and light box integration & test
  • FQC final inspection — Full check of appearance, structure and electrical
  • Packing & shipping — Totem loading, overseas installation guidance
